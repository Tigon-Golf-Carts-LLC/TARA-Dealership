Explore Canada’s Golf Course Rankings and Emerging Trends in 2025

In North America, golf is more than just a sport; it’s a lifestyle. With the arrival of 2025, the ranking of Canadian golf courses has become a hot topic for golfers and travelers. Whether searching for the top 10 public golf courses in Canada or planning to experience the best public golf courses in Canada, you’ll find that Canadian courses continue to lead the way in design, environment, and service. Furthermore, with the trend towards environmental protection and smart golf courses, more and more courses are incorporating electric transportation. Professional electric golf carts provided by Tara are becoming a crucial part of enhancing the course experience.

Factors Influencing Canada’s Golf Course Rankings

The ranking of Canadian golf courses is not solely based on size and reputation; it’s more about the overall experience:

Course Design and Difficulty

Many top-ranked courses are designed by world-renowned designers, exemplified by their integration of natural topography and strategic layouts.

Natural Scenery and Ecological Conservation

From courses on the Atlantic coast to those nestled in the Rocky Mountains, Canada’s golf courses are renowned for their unique natural backdrops.

Services and Amenities

The quality of the clubhouse, dining, training facilities, and even golf carts all factor into the rankings.

Sustainability and Environmental Protection

More and more courses are prioritizing environmentally friendly energy and management practices, such as opting for electric golf carts to reduce carbon emissions.

Among these factors, the Tara electric golf cart perfectly aligns with the development trend of modern golf courses, providing players and managers with a quiet, environmentally friendly, and efficient travel experience.

Canadian Open Courses to Watch in 2025

Based on industry trends and golfer feedback, the following courses are frequently mentioned in the Canadian Golf Course Rankings, and almost always make the top 10 public golf courses in Canada or the best public golf courses in Canada 2025 lists:

Cabot Cliffs, Nova Scotia

A long-standing top-ranked public course in Canada, known for its stunning coastal cliffs and challenging fairways, it is a dream destination for many golfers.

Cabot Links, Nova Scotia

Owned by the same resort as Cabot Cliffs, it is renowned for its traditional links-style design, seamlessly integrating with the natural landscape of the Atlantic coast.

Fairmont Jasper Park Lodge Golf Course, Alberta

Located in Rocky Mountain National Park, it has earned high acclaim for its lake-and-forest setting and unique natural experience.

Fairmont Banff Springs Golf Course, Alberta

This historic course, consistently ranked as one of Canada’s best public golf courses in 2025, is renowned for its stunning mountain scenery and unique terrain design.

Cape Breton Highlands Links, Nova Scotia

This classic course, blending mountain and coastal elements, is considered one of Canada’s most traditional public courses.

Tobiano Golf Course, British Columbia

Located in the Thompson River Valley, it is renowned for its stunning highland scenery and strategically designed course, and has been a rapidly rising star in recent years.

Bigwin Island Golf Club, Ontario

This unique island course, accessible by boat, offers both a challenging and unique experience, making it one of Ontario’s most iconic golf destinations.

Links at Crowbush Cove, Prince Edward Island

PEI’s most prestigious course, featuring a traditional links design, is popular with both local and international players.

Greywolf Golf Course, British Columbia

Located in the resort region of the Rockies, Greywolf Golf Course is renowned for its iconic “Cliffhanger” sixth hole and consistently ranks highly in Canada’s golf course rankings.

Royal Ontario Golf Club, Ontario

As one of the public golf courses, Royal Ontario combines wide fairways with challenging greens, attracting numerous golfers.

These courses not only stand out for their landscape and design, but also for their continuous improvements in management and facilities. For example, more and more courses are introducing electric golf carts to promote green operations. Tara electric golf carts are highly sought after for their environmental friendliness, smart features, and comfort, making them an ideal choice for many top-ranked courses.

Popular Questions

1. What makes a top-ranked golf course in Canada?

Top-ranked courses in Canada often combine landscape, design, and service. For example, strategic designs that incorporate natural scenery, combined with environmentally friendly and intelligent amenities, can help them stand out in rankings.

2. Which provinces will have the best public golf courses in Canada by 2025?

Nova Scotia and Alberta are the most representative, with courses in the coastal and Rocky Mountain regions, in particular, often appearing at the top of Canada’s golf course rankings.

3. How does sustainability affect golf course rankings in Canada?

As environmental protection becomes a social consensus, sustainable practices at golf courses are becoming increasingly important. The use of electric carts, energy-efficient irrigation systems, and reduced chemical use are all key factors influencing rankings. Tara electric golf carts offer green solutions to this trend.

4. Why are electric golf carts important for Canadian golf courses?

Canada’s terrain is diverse and environmentally sensitive, and traditional fuel vehicles can pose noise and emission issues. Electric vehicles not only reduce maintenance costs but also provide a more comfortable experience for players. As a professional manufacturer, Tara’s products have been recognized internationally.
