Balbriggan Golf Club Adopts Tara Electric Golf Carts

Balbriggan Golf Club in Ireland has recently taken a significant step toward modernization and sustainability by introducing a new fleet of Tara electric golf carts. Since the fleet’s arrival earlier this year, the results have been outstanding — improved member satisfaction, higher operational efficiency, and a noticeable boost in revenue.

A Smarter, Greener Fleet Choice

Balbriggan Golf Club, a well-established 18-hole course known for its warm community and scenic layout, was looking for a modern fleet solution that combined comfort, performance, and sustainability. After careful evaluation, the club chose Tara, a leading manufacturer of lithium-powered golf carts trusted by golf courses worldwide.

According to the club representative:

“Members are full of praise for the Tara buggy, citing the features, height and comfort. Since we introduced the Tara earlier this year, we can now accommodate additional demand due to the capacity of the lithium batteries. Revenue is consequently up also.”

This feedback perfectly summarizes what Tara stands for — better design, better performance, and better business outcomes.

Comfort Meets Performance

Tara’s electric golf carts are designed with both golfers and operators in mind. The elevated seating position and ergonomic layout ensures maximum comfort throughout the game. Members also appreciate the quiet ride and smooth handling, which enhance the overall golfing experience.

Powered by advanced lithium battery technology, the fleet delivers consistent performance throughout the day, allowing the club to serve more players without frequent charging or downtime. Compared with traditional lead-acid batteries, Tara’s lithium systems are more efficient, maintenance-free, and environmentally friendly.

Driving Efficiency and Revenue

The upgrade has allowed Balbriggan Golf Club to expand its rental capacity, meeting increased player demand during peak hours. With fewer maintenance issues and longer-lasting power, the fleet operates with higher uptime — directly contributing to increased revenue and smoother daily management.

This success story illustrates how an investment in modern electric golf carts can yield both operational and financial benefits for golf clubs. Tara’s fleets are not only energy-efficient but also built to last, ensuring long-term value and reliability.

Committed to Sustainable Golf Mobility

By adopting Tara’s electric carts, Balbriggan joins a growing number of clubs worldwide choosing sustainable solutions to reduce their carbon footprint. Tara’s quiet, zero-emission vehicles align perfectly with the peaceful nature of golf courses while helping clubs meet modern environmental goals.

From design to performance, Tara continues to redefine what a modern golf cart should be — stylish, durable, and sustainable.

About Tara

Tara is a global manufacturer of premium electric golf carts and utility vehicles, offering innovative lithium technology and smart fleet solutions for golf courses, resorts, and private communities. With decades of experience and a strong focus on sustainability, Tara is driving the future of golf mobility — greener, smarter, and better.
