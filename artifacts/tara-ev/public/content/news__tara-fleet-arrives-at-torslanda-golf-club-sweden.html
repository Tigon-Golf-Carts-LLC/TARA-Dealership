Tara Fleet Arrives at Torslanda Golf Club, Sweden

Torslanda Golf Club in Sweden has recently upgraded its golf cart fleet, introducing 12 Tara Spirit golf carts and 2 Tara Turfman utility carts. This move further optimizes the club’s cart lineup for both passenger transport and operational tasks.

The 12 delivered Tara Spirit carts are equipped with the Tara GPS Fleet Management System, enabling the management team to monitor cart usage more efficiently. Meanwhile, the new Turfman utility carts will be used for course maintenance, equipment transport, and daily logistics, providing staff with carts better suited to their operational needs.

New Fleet Configuration

 

Carts at a golf club serve various roles. Transporting players, providing member services, managing tournaments, and maintaining the turf all require different cart capabilities.

Torslanda Golf Club has adopted a more distinct cart configuration strategy:

Tara Spirit Golf Carts: Intended for daily use by members and visitors, offering a comfortable and stable on-course transport experience;

Tara Turfman Utility carts: Used by the maintenance team to transport tools, equipment, and materials, enhancing logistical flexibility.

By distinguishing between passenger and utility carts, the club minimizes conflicts between different use cases. For instance, maintenance staff no longer need to use carts intended for members to carry out transport tasks, and the club can schedule cart usage more rationally based on specific functions.

 

GPS Fleet Management

 

A key feature of this project is that all 12 Tara Spirit golf carts are equipped with the Tara GPS Fleet Management System.

For the management team, fleet management involves more than just tracking cart locations; it also encompasses usage efficiency, battery status, and operational performance.

The Tara GPS system supports features such as real-time cart location tracking, GPS geofence management, battery SoC monitoring, cart speed monitoring, and usage history tracking. Managers can view cart locations in real-time, track usage frequency, and establish zone-based management rules tailored to operational needs.

For example, during tournaments or peak hours, the management team can optimize dispatching based on cart distribution. During daily operations, usage records help assess cart workloads, providing data to support future maintenance and procurement decisions.

 

Designed for Course Operations

 

When purchasing carts, golf clubs must consider a wide range of factors. The Tara Spirit golf cart utilizes proven LiFePO4 lithium battery technology to deliver consistent, reliable power.

Tara golf carts offer a wide range of configuration options—including seating, accessories, and functional customizations—enabling golf courses of all types to build a fleet tailored to their specific operational needs.

Designed specifically for course operations, the Tara Turfman utility cart is built for maintenance and transport tasks. It handles equipment handling, material transport, and daily inspections, providing golf course staff with greater operational efficiency.
