Discovering Golf Course Excellence in the UK: Tradition Meets Innovation

With over 2,000 venues, the golf course UK landscape offers history, diversity, and cutting-edge tech like golf cart GPS systems to enhance every round.

What Makes Golf in the UK Unique?

Golf in the UK isn’t just a sport—it’s a tradition deeply woven into the country’s landscape. From the windswept links of Scotland to the manicured parklands in southern England, UK golf courses offer varied terrain, world-class design, and a strong sense of heritage. The country is home to many iconic venues, including St Andrews, Royal Birkdale, and Wentworth.

However, beyond tradition, modern facilities across the UK are investing in innovation. For instance, many golf clubs are integrating golf cart GPS system solutions to offer players a more seamless and informed game.

How Many Golf Courses Are There in the UK?

The UK is one of the most golf-rich countries globally, boasting approximately 2,270 golf courses. England has the most, followed by Scotland, Ireland, and Wales. The abundance of options makes it an ideal destination for both domestic golfers and international visitors.

In recent years, the demand for efficient on-course mobility and smarter navigation has increased. As a result, modern golf cart GPS systems have become standard in many premium clubs, offering yardage data, hazard mapping, and pace-of-play tools—all accessible through integrated systems like Tara’s Smart Display.

Which Are the Best Golf Courses in the UK?

Selecting the best golf courses in the UK is subjective, but several consistently rank high in global ratings. Here are just a few:

  • St Andrews (Old Course), Scotland – Known as the “Home of Golf.”
  • Royal County Down, Northern Ireland – A blend of challenge and breathtaking views.
  • Sunningdale (Old), England – Elegant layout just outside London.
  • Royal Dornoch, Scotland – A hidden gem on the Scottish Highlands.
  • Wentworth Club, England – Host of the PGA Championship.

To elevate the player experience, many of these elite courses are exploring or already using smart GPS for golf carts and quiet electric utility carts like the Tara Spirit Plus, ensuring a more enjoyable and uninterrupted round.

Why Is GPS for Golf Carts Gaining Popularity?

As golf course operations become more data-driven, GPS for golf carts plays a crucial role in modernizing course management. These systems provide:

  • Real-time yardage and course maps
  • Speed monitoring and pace control
  • Two-way communication between players and clubhouse
  • Enhanced safety and route management

Tara’s GPS-enabled carts offer these features through intuitive touchscreen displays, integrated within their premium cart lineup. Whether it’s a golf course in the UK, a resort in Spain, or a community course in France, these solutions help courses offer a refined and tech-savvy experience.

How Are UK Golf Courses Adapting to Sustainability and Efficiency?

As the UK focuses more on environmental responsibility, golf courses are adopting electric transportation and energy-efficient practices. Electric buggies from Tara are a key part of this movement.

Models like the Tara Spirit Plus and Turfman 700 EEC are popular among European courses seeking both performance and EEC road-legal compliance. They combine quiet operation, low emissions, and advanced control systems to support sustainable operations across the UK’s most prestigious venues.

Choosing Tara for Your UK Golf Course Fleet

UK clubs looking to modernize their fleets with premium electric golf carts should consider Tara’s range. As a European-friendly brand, Tara combines elegant design, engineering excellence, and advanced GPS capabilities—all backed by strong after-sales support.

Whether you’re managing a championship venue or upgrading a boutique course, Tara offers solutions tailored to your terrain, budget, and player expectations.
