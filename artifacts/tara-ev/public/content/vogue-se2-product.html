Tara VOGUE SE2 offers a luxurious golfing experience with its sleek design and efficient lithium-ion battery. Its comfort is elevated by all-climate leather seats and a protective canopy. Paired with ample storage, LED lighting, and stylish 12-inch wheels, it's both stylish and practical.

The All New VOGUE SE2

The all new VOGUE SE2 features bold new colors and new body styling, it’s more luxurious, and offers more value than ever. Built-in new features and equipment designed for player comfort and convenience to enhance the driving experience.

Vehicle Highlights

ALL-CLIMATE LUXURY SEAT

These leather seats make it easier to relax and enjoy the ride, whether around the neighborhood or to the nearby golf course.

INJECTION MOULD CANOPY

The injection mould canopy as  a reliable part that can withstand repeated use without frequent repairs or replacement and drains well.

LARGE BAGWELL

Our new larger, more spacious bagwell holds two staff bags comfortably and securely.

LED HEADLIGHTS

Light your path with the energy-efficient LED headlights and taillights, now standard on the TARA 2 Passenger.

12‘’ ALUMINUM WHEEL 205/50R12 TIRE

Make the neighbors envious with premium tires and wheels that add another level of customization.

CADDIE STAND

Elegantly crafted in deluxe black, this caddie stand boasts high-grade, fade-resistant cushions that resist mold. Securely fastened with a four-point system, it offers a broad and stable space to stand.
