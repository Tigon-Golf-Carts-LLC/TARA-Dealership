Tara Harmony is a fusion of luxury and efficiency, featuring all-climate easy-to-clean seats and a durable injection mould canopy. Its spacious design includes a large bagwell and energy-efficient LED lighting, complemented by stylish 8-inch iron wheels. An adjustable steering wheel ensures tailored driving comfort and control on the greens.

DRIVE IN STYLE AND COMFORT WITH HARMONY

Whether you’re exploring the course or taking a break between holes, enjoy the luxury seating, smooth ride and modern amenities our golf carts offer. Harmony will give you a unique golfing memory.

Vehicle Highlights

SEAT & ALUMINUM FRAME

These seats are made of breathable foam padding, soft and doubly long sitting without fatigue, adding better comfort to your ride, and easy to clean as well. The aluminum frame makes the cart lighter and more corrosion-resistant.

DASHBOARD AND ADJUSTABLE STEERING COLUMN

The adjustable steering column can be adjusted to the perfect angle to suit different drivers, enhancing comfort and control. The dashboard integrates multiple storage spaces, control switches, and USB charging ports, providing convenience and functionality at your fingertips.

CADDIE STAND AND GOLF BAG RACK

Securely fastened with a four-point system, the caddie stand offers a broad and stable space to stand. A golf cart bag rack keeps your bag secure with straps that can be adjusted and tightened making your clubs easily accessible.

SCORECARD HOLDER

Centrally located on the steering wheel, this holder features a top clip to securely hold most golf scorecards. Its expansive surface ensures ample room for both writing and reading.

8‘’ ALUMINUM WHEEL

Say goodbye to noise distractions! Whether you're driving on the street or on the golf course, the quiet operation of our tires ensures that you'll enjoy a peaceful driving experience.

STORAGE COMPARTMENT WITH GOLF BALL & TEE HOLDER

The storage compartment is designed to hold your personal belongings securely and includes a dedicated space for golf balls and tees. This ensures that your items remain organized and no longer roll around randomly.
