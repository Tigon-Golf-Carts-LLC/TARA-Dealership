Effortless Driving on the Green: Choosing the Right Golf Cart in the UK

Golf carts aren’t just for the fairway anymore. In the UK, they’re becoming popular for resorts, estates, and even light road use. Here’s what to consider.

What is a golf cart and how is it used?

A golf cart is a small electric or gas-powered vehicle primarily designed to carry golfers and their equipment across a golf course. In the UK, golf carts are commonly seen at private clubs, leisure parks, holiday resorts, and even large private estates where quiet, eco-friendly transport is preferred.

Besides sports and recreation, many buyers today are exploring carts golf carts for use in caravan parks, care communities, and urban areas where low-speed travel makes sense. The versatility and low noise of electric carts make them ideal for both private and semi-public environments.

What does “cart” mean in golf?

In the context of golf, a “cart” refers to a two- or four-seater vehicle designed to transport players around the course. It improves game flow and reduces fatigue. However, a cart in golf is not just a convenience — it’s also part of the modern golfing experience. Some high-end clubs in the UK now offer premium electric carts as part of their luxury services, featuring GPS, lithium batteries, and all-weather tires.

Can you use a golf cart on UK roads?

Yes, golf carts can be used on public roads in the UK, but only if they meet certain regulatory requirements. Most importantly, the vehicle must be EEC certified — that is, it must comply with the European Economic Community (EEC) standards for road-legal low-speed vehicles. These standards cover lighting, speed restrictions (generally under 25 mph), safety equipment, mirrors, turn signals, and more.

Without this certification, golf carts are legally restricted to private land or designated areas such as golf clubs and resorts. Models like the Turfman 700 EEC from Tara, for example, are fully EEC-compliant and legally allowed to operate on certain public roads within the UK, especially in slow-traffic zones or private communities with road permissions.

Before taking your golf cart for sale out on public roads, always check with local transport authorities and confirm registration, insurance, and vehicle classification rules.

How much does a golf cart cost in the UK?

Prices can vary significantly depending on the type of cart, battery technology, seating configuration, and whether it’s road legal. A basic two-seater electric cart for private golf use might start at around £4,000–£5,000. More advanced models with lithium batteries, high-end finishes, and EEC certification can reach £8,000–£12,000 or more.

It’s also worth noting that premium brands, such as Tara’s range of golf carts, offer customization options, extended warranties, and dealer support across Europe and the UK, making them an appealing choice for clubs and private buyers alike.

What are the different types of golf carts?

UK buyers can choose from several types of carts depending on usage:

  • Electric Golf Carts – Ideal for most applications, low noise and eco-friendly.

  • Gas-powered Golf Carts – Less common in the UK due to emissions but still used in rugged outdoor environments.

  • Two-Seaters vs. Four-Seaters – Larger carts are ideal for resorts or family use.

  • Street-Legal (EEC Certified) – Road-worthy versions with lights, mirrors, turn indicators, and VIN numbers.

  • Utility Golf Carts – Equipped with cargo beds for use on farms, estates, or maintenance teams.

Each of these models serves different needs, and choosing the right one depends on your environment and how often you plan to use it outside the course.
