Over the last decade, golf cars have evolved from niche vehicles used strictly on the golf course to versatile transport solutions for resorts, retirement communities, commercial properties, and private estates. What Is a Golf Car?

A golf car, often used interchangeably with golf cart, is a compact motorised vehicle primarily designed to transport golfers and their equipment around a course. However, modern golf cars are now widely used for:

  • Large private properties
  • Event venues and festivals
  • University and business campuses
  • Resorts and retirement communities
  • Eco-conscious transport in urban zones

Golf cars come in various configurations—from 2-seaters for course play to 4 seater golf carts or even 6-seaters for family or shuttle use.

Gas vs Electric Golf Cart: Which Is Right for You?

The most common question among buyers: gas or electric golf cart? Let’s break it down:

Electric Golf Cars

Pros:

  • Quiet, emission-free operation
  • Lower maintenance and fuel costs
  • Ideal for short-distance, frequent use
  • Great for indoor or semi-enclosed environments

Cons:

  • Limited range (typically 20–40 miles per charge)
  • Requires charging infrastructure
  • Battery replacement costs every few years

Explore electric golf buggies for sale that are efficient and environmentally friendly.

⛽ Gas Golf Cars

Pros:

  • Longer range and higher torque
  • Faster refuelling—no charging downtime
  • Better suited for heavy-duty or hilly terrain

Cons:

  • Louder engine noise
  • Emissions and fuel expenses
  • Higher long-term maintenance

Verdict: For daily short trips or quiet environments, electric is ideal. For rugged use or longer distances, a gas golf cart might serve you better.

How to Choose the Best Golf Carts

Choosing the best golf carts depends on more than price. Consider:

  • Use case: Golf course, private estate, business use?
  • Capacity: Do you need a 2, 4 or 6-seater model?
  • Road legality: Some golf cars can be modified for street use
  • Terrain: Flat or hilly? Paved or off-road?
  • Maintenance support: Are parts and service readily available?

FAQs from EU Golf Cart Buyers

Are electric golf cars street legal in the UK?

Not by default. However, some models can be modified to meet local regulations for low-speed electric vehicles (EEC). They’ll need lights, indicators, mirrors, and registration.

How long does a golf cart battery last?

With proper maintenance, most golf cart batteries last 4–6 years. Lithium-ion batteries can last longer and charge faster, but are more expensive upfront.

Is it worth buying a 4 seater golf cart for home use?

Yes—especially for large private properties, estates, or community developments. 4 seater golf carts are ideal for transporting family or guests comfortably.
