Tara Explorer 2+2 is a lifted golf cart designed for those who need more than just smooth fairways. Featuring a raised chassis and off-road tread tires, this personal golf cart handles varied terrain with ease while delivering quiet, energy-efficient performance. From course paths to community trails, it offers a stable, comfortable ride with elevated style and utility.

UNPARALLELED OFF-ROAD ADVENTURES AWAIT

The unique vehicle design is crafted to elevate your driving experience with comfortable seats, off-road tires, and efficient lithium batteries. Take an adventure trip with your family or friends at any time.

Vehicle Highlights

COMFORTABLE LUXURY SEATS

TARA’s luxury seats are exceptionally well-rounded, catering to comfort, protection, and aesthetic appeal. Crafted from soft-touch imitation leather with an elegant carved pattern, they ensure a luxurious experience whether you're cruising for personal transit or leisure.

CUBOID SOUND BAR

The system allows for seamless wireless connectivity through the screen, enhancing its usability and convenience. Additionally, it features adjustable light modes; the speaker lights pulsate in sync with the music, creating an engaging atmosphere that enhances every tune.

CARPLAY

The Tara Explorer 2+2 golf cart offers integrated CarPlay, bringing your favorite iPhone features right to the touchscreen. With CarPlay, you can manage your music, get turn-by-turn directions, and handle calls easily, all through the cart’s display. Whether you're on the golf course or out for a relaxed ride, CarPlay keeps everything at your fingertips. Plus, with Android Auto compatibility, Android users can enjoy the same connectivity and control.

FLIP-FLOP REAR SEAT & STORAGE KIT

Enhance passenger comfort and convenience with our rear armrest that includes cup holders. Additionally, our flip-flop rear seat comes equipped with a handrail and footrest, providing enhanced stability and comfort, while the storage box located underneath the seat maximizes space efficiency.

FRONT BUMPER AND ALL LED LIGHTS

The heavy-duty front bumper provides effective protection. Led brake lights and turning signals allow you to drive smoothly even in the dark, like a beast dominating the night.

SILENT TIRES WITH OFF-ROAD THREAD

This cool-looking tire is designed for off-road use and can adapt to a variety of terrains. Its silent texture design reduces the noise generated by the vehicle during driving and enhances the grip ability. All to make your driving more fun.
