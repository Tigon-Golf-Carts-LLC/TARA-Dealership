Spirit Plus – Premium Electric Golf Cart for Golf Courses

Experience a luxury electric golf cart built for today’s golf course needs — smooth, quiet, and packed with value. Designed for fleet use, this golf course cart offers premium comfort, stylish design, and extra entertainment features, delivering a high-end feel at a high-value golf car price.

RIDING WITH ELECTRIC ELEGANCE

Tara Spirit Plus promises an unparalleled journey with its energy-efficient electric prowess. Experience silky smooth acceleration and unmatched hill-climbing capabilities that redefine expectations. Making battery power synonymous with horsepower, it ensures players enjoy a seamless ride that feels like a glide.

Vehicle Highlights

ALL-CLIMATE LUXURY SEAT

These specially customized luxury leather seats make it easy to relax and enjoy the ride whether on the greens or around the neighborhood. Designed for superior comfort, they offer excellent support with wrapping and shock absorption.

COMFORT GRIP STEERING WHEEL

The steering wheel features a comfortable grip and responsive handling, complete with a convenient scorecard holder and pencil slot. The adjustable steering wheel is meticulously designed to enhance driving ease and provide the driver with optimal control over their driving view and the distance to the wheel.

MULTI-FUNCTIONAL DASHBOARD

Tara’s refined exterior and contemporary interior enhance your golfing experience. The redesigned interior minimizes noise and prevents sliding, accommodating drinks, tees, golfbags, cellphones, and gloves. Tara also keeps you informed about the golf cart’s status,ensuring a seamless and informed golfing experience.

ACCESSORIES AT THE REAR

Tara has flowing contours that are crafted in your choice of premium materials, depending on how you wish to configure your golf cart or accentuate the interior. A wide array of add-on accessories available for both golfing and personal use including Golf ball washer, golf bag holder, sand bottle, caddy master cooler.

CUBOID SOUND BAR WITH LIGHTS

Music is an essential part of any leisure moment, and this sleek, cuboid sound bar delivers outstanding audio quality. With its rhythmic  lights, you can enjoy your favorite tunes anytime, anywhere, creating the perfect ambiance for every occasion.

12" ALUMINUM WHEEL WITH RADIAL TIRE

Experience the perfect blend of style and performance with our 12” alloy wheel tires. Crafted for golf course excellence, these tires provide outstanding water dispersion, traction, and cornering capabilities. The lightweight, durable design respects the delicate greens while offering superior handling.
