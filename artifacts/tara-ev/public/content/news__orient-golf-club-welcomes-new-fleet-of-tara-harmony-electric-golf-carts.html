Orient Golf Club Welcomes New Fleet of Tara Harmony Electric Golf Carts

Tara, a leading innovator in electric golf cart solutions for the golf and leisure industries, has delivered 80 units of its flagship Harmony electric golf fleet carts to Orient Golf Club in Southeast Asia. This delivery underscores both Tara’s and Orient Golf Club’s commitment to eco-friendly practices and exceptional player experiences.

The decision by Orient Golf Club to adopt Tara’s Harmony golf carts reflects a shared commitment to sustainability and innovation. Designed for maximum efficiency and minimal environmental impact, the Harmony model combines sleek aesthetics with advanced battery technology, ensuring a smooth and quiet ride while reducing carbon emissions on the course.

Each Harmony cart is crafted with an all-aluminum alloy frame for enhanced durability and fitted with easy-to-clean seats, a practical choice for high-traffic facilities. Tara’s self-developed high-performance lithium batteries, along with the optional golf course management system and GPS functionality, further streamline operations for Orient Golf Club, enhancing convenience and efficiency for both staff and players.

Orient Golf Club selected the Harmony fleet not only for its reputation as a high-performance, low-maintenance cart but also for its modern features that align with the club’s emphasis on player comfort and environmental stewardship. With these 80 new Harmony carts, Orient Golf Club is poised to offer an elevated experience that mirrors its dedication to excellence and innovation.

“We are thrilled to collaborate with Orient Golf Club, a respected name in quality and innovation in the golf community,” stated Mr. Tony, President of Tara. “This partnership resonates with Tara’s vision of advancing sustainable mobility solutions within the global golfing community.”

Tara’s expansion into the Asian market, coupled with the increasing demand for sustainable transportation options, demonstrates the global shift towards electric solutions within the leisure and recreation industries. The new Tara Harmony golf carts will be available for member and guest use at Orient Golf Club starting now.

Tara is an industry leader in electric vehicle solutions, specializing in high-quality golf and utility vehicles that prioritize performance, sustainability, and innovation.
