How Much Does a Golf Cart Weigh? A Complete 2025 Guide

Wondering how much a golf cart weighs and what affects it? This guide breaks down standard weights, battery influence, trailer capacity, and how weight impacts performance.

What Is the Average Weight of a Golf Cart?

The average golf cart weight typically falls between 900 to 1,200 pounds (408 to 544 kg) without passengers or extra cargo. However, the exact number varies depending on several factors:

  • Power Type: Electric carts with lead-acid batteries are heavier than those with lithium batteries.
  • Seating Capacity: A 4-seater or 6-seater model will weigh significantly more than a compact 2-seater.
  • Materials Used: Aluminum frames (used in premium models like those from Tara Golf Cart) reduce weight without compromising strength.

For example, Tara’s Spirit Plus weighs approximately 950–1050 lbs depending on the battery configuration.

How Much Does an Electric Golf Cart Weigh with Batteries?

Battery type has a huge influence on a golf cart’s total weight:

  • Lead-acid batteries can add over 300 lbs to the vehicle.
  • Lithium batteries, like the 105Ah or 160Ah options offered by Tara, are significantly lighter and more efficient.

A cart equipped with Tara’s 160Ah LiFePO4 battery may weigh around 980–1,050 lbs, depending on features. This weight savings translates into better energy efficiency, handling, and reduced trailer strain.

Can You Tow a Golf Cart with a Trailer?

Yes—but you must match your trailer’s capacity with your cart’s gross vehicle weight (GVW), which includes:

  • The cart itself
  • Battery system
  • Accessories and cargo

For instance, a golf cart like the Tara Explorer 2+2, which includes off-road tires and a lifted chassis, weighs around 1,200 lbs, so the trailer should support at least 1,500 lbs GVW.

Always check the ramp angle and secure the cart properly during transit.

Does Weight Impact Golf Cart Speed and Range?

Absolutely. A heavier cart will generally:

  • Accelerate slower
  • Consume more battery power
  • Require more frequent charging

That’s why many golf course operators now prefer lightweight lithium-powered golf carts. Tara’s aluminum frame construction and lithium battery system improve power-to-weight ratio, extending driving range by up to 20–30%.

What Is the Lightest Golf Cart You Can Buy?

If weight is your top priority—for trailering, speed, or terrain—consider lightweight electric models:

  • 2-seaters without accessories
  • Lithium battery-equipped carts
  • Compact chassis with aluminum body

The T1 Series from Tara is a great example, designed for low maintenance and nimble handling, with total weight under 950 lbs depending on configuration.

Why Golf Cart Weight Matters

Whether you’re transporting, storing, or simply trying to maximize battery performance, knowing your golf cart’s weight helps in many ways:

  • Choosing the right trailer or hauler
  • Optimizing battery usage and terrain capabilities
  • Complying with road or resort regulations

With options like Tara’s Spirit Plus or Explorer 2+2, you can balance performance, weight, and durability for your specific needs.

Golf cart weight depends on power system, materials, seating, and features. Brands like Tara Golf Cart offer modern, lightweight electric vehicles using lithium batteries and aluminum frames—helping reduce total weight while enhancing performance.
