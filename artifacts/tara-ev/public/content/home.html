About Tara Golf Cart About Tara Golf Cart

For nearly two decades, Tara has been redefining the golf cart experience — combining cutting-edge engineering, luxury design, and sustainable power systems. From famous golf courses to exclusive estates and modern communities, our electric golf carts deliver unmatched reliability, performance, and style.

Every Tara golf cart is thoughtfully crafted — from energy-efficient lithium systems to integrated fleet solutions tailored for professional golf course operations.

At Tara, we don’t just build electric golf carts — we build trust, elevate experiences, and drive the future of sustainable mobility.
