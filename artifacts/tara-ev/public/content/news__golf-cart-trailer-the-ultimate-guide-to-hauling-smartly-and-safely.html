Golf Cart Trailer: The Ultimate Guide to Hauling Smartly and Safely

A golf cart trailer expands the versatility of your cart, allowing you to transport cargo, tools, or even another cart. With the right golf cart trailer hitch and setup, you unlock new practical capabilities for residential, commercial, and leisure uses.

What Exactly Is a Golf Cart Trailer?

A golf cart trailer is a lightweight, towable platform designed to attach behind a golf cart via a hitch. Trailers come in several configurations—utility beds for landscaping, cargo boxes for resorts, or flatbeds for lodging. Tara offers model-specific compatibility for accessories, ensuring a seamless fit and reliable performance.

Why Use a Trailer for Golf Cart?

  1. Carry More Cargo
    Ideal for hauling tools, luggage, golf bags, maintenance gear, or event supplies—without cluttering the cart cabin.

  2. Support Multiple Vehicles
    Whether transporting another cart or towing lightweight equipment like floor sweepers, a trailer for golf cart increases fleet efficiency.

  3. Improve Operational Flow
    In resorts, campuses, or parks, trailers reduce the number of trips needed—saving time and labor.

  4. Expand Usage Scenarios
    Garden maintenance, construction sites, airport shuttles, and even campground logistics can be streamlined with a trailer-equipped cart.

Must-Have: Golf Cart Trailer Hitch

The link between cart and trailer, a golf cart trailer hitch must be sturdy and easy to install. Hitches bolt directly onto the chassis. High-quality options, when paired with a receiver and safety chains, ensure stable towing.

For example, steel hitches from accessories brands can be mounted on Club Car, EZ-GO, Yamaha, and Tara models using standard bolt kits.

Common Questions Answered About Golf Cart Trailers

1. Can golf carts safely tow trailers?

Yes—with the right setup. Most electric carts can tow light trailers as long as the load stays within capacity. Reddit users emphasize that towing at high road speeds with wheels on the ground may damage brakes or gearboxes Reddit. Always match load weight to vehicle capabilities and ensure balanced hitching.

2. What trailer types work best?

According to CartFinder’s guide, choices include:

  • Enclosed trailers: provide protection from weather and debris

  • Flatbed trailers with ramps: ideal for cart transport

  • Open utility trailers with drop-down ramps: balanced, cost-effective

Weight capacity, ramp platforms, and tie-downs are key trailer specs to check.

3. How do I secure a golf cart to a trailer?

Proper tie-down techniques matter. Recommendations include:

  • Secure from frame—not tires

  • Use multiple straps across front and rear

  • Chocks beneath wheels prevent movement
    Forum users specifically suggest strapping seat bottoms and roofs.

Building Your Own Golf Cart Trailer System

  1. Choose the trailer
    Define your usage—enclosed, flatbed, folding ramp, or utility bed with side walls.

  2. Install a quality hitch
    Select a steel or aluminum golf cart trailer hitch kit compatible with your model. Bolt it securely to the frame.

  3. Add a receiver and safety chain
    Attach a locking receiver sleeve and use at least one safety chain.

  4. Select suitable tie-downs
    Ratchet straps with soft loops avoid puncturing trim. Ensure even load distribution.

  5. Load and test
    Begin with light cargo to verify weight balance and stopping power before full loading.

Legal Considerations and Safety

  • Speed and terrain limits: Trailers should only be used on private roads or designated service routes—not highways.

  • Tune vehicle capacity: Know your cart’s tow rating (typically 500–800 lb).

  • Inspect regularly: Check chassis bolts, trailer connections, wires, and strap security before each use.

Tara’s Compatibility & Custom Add-Ons

Tara supports trailer use with optional hitch and light kits. Accessories include:

  • Hitch kits with receiver/tow ball

  • Cargo trailers sized for utility use

  • Weather-resistant utility beds

  • Trailer wiring harnesses to connect brake- and tail-lights

These options make upgrading to a trailer-ready system easier and more reliable.

Maintenance Tips for Trailer Setup

  • Lubricate hitch pins and joints every few months

  • Inspect tie-downs for wear and replace frayed straps

  • Check trailer tires for pressure and tread

  • Test light connections monthly to maintain visibility

These checks ensure safety and prolong service life across both cart and trailer components.

Final Words on Golf Cart Trailers

Adding a golf cart trailer transforms a simple cart into a multifunctional asset—ready for landscaping, utility missions, or light towing. To ensure success:

  • Choose the right golf cart trailer hitch

  • Match trailer capacity to cart performance

  • Follow secure transport practices

  • Keep hitches and tie-downs well maintained
