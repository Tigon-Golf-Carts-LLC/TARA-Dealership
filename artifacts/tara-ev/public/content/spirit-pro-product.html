AN ALL-IN-ONE ELECTRIC SOLUTION

The Spirit Pro seamlessly blends luxury and innovation to deliver the ultimate on-course experience. Featuring an energy-efficient battery, ergonomic design, and sleek aesthetics, it ensures smooth rides and standout style on the greens. With ample storage and 8-inch wheels, its functionality and appeal are further elevated.

Vehicle Highlights

EASY-TO-CLEAN INTEGRATED SEAT

The newly designed, easy-to-clean seats offer a superb riding experience. Their seamless surface design ensures convenient daily cleaning and maintenance, while the durable construction withstands various weather conditions and provide consistent support. Additionally, the seats come with safety handrails to ensure your safety while riding.

COMFORT GRIP STEERING WHEEL

The steering wheel featuring a comfortable grip and responsive handling with scorecard holder. Even your pencil has its place. Its adjustable steering wheel is meticulously designed to enhance driving ease and provide the driver with optimal control over their driving view and the distance to the wheel. The ergonomic design ensures a comfortable grip, giving you precise control over every maneuver.

STORAGE COMPARTMENT

The overhead storage compartment makes it easy to store your gloves, caps, towels, and more things. The clever design makes it integrated into the roof. Just reach out and get whatever you want.

CUP HOLDER

The cup holder with an integrated structure can be used to store your drinks. The hollow design at the bottom can drain excess liquid and keep the cup holder clean and dry. Bring your coffee & cola and enjoy the game.

MODERNIZED FRONT END & DASH

Our custom-designed and manufactured front cover boasts an impressive, unique, and futuristic appearance. The front cover and lampshade are easy to disassemble, and the internal wiring is reserved, which can be easily customized and equipped with headlights.

8‘’ ALUMINUM WHEEL

Navigate the greens effortlessly with our specially-designed tires. Fitted with 8-inch aluminum rims, they’re not just about good looks.  Designed thoughtfully, their flat tread ensures the greens remain undamaged. Experience a seamless ride, regardless of the terrain.
