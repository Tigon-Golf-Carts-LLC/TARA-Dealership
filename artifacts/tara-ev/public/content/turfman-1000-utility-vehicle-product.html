Turfman 1000 – High-Capacity Utility Vehicle

Powertrains

ELiTE Lithium

Turfman 1000 boasts an impressive towing capacity, ample storage space, and a durable construction that can handle the rigors of golf courses, sports fields, and large estates. Its intuitive design and user-friendly features make it the perfect solution for groundskeepers and maintenance teams looking to streamline their operations and get the job done faster, all while prioritizing sustainability.

BUILT TO HAUL. READY FOR ANYTHING.

With a rear flatbed that is ideal for carrying multiple loads in a single ride, the Turfman 1000 is expertly crafted with the demands of contemporary transportation challenges in mind. Turfman 1000's spacious flatbed guarantees efficiency whether you're transporting landscaping equipment, handling event supplies, or delivering items to various locations within a large facility. Its design guarantees that space is never a constraint and demonstrates an understanding of the diverse requirements of cargo transport.

Vehicle Highlights

CARGO BOX

Got heavy gear to move? The Turfman 1000 is equipped with this tough thermoplastic cargo box, mounted on the back for extra hauling power. Whether you're headed to the farm, the woods, or the shore, it's the perfect companion for tools, bags, and everything in between.

DASHBOARD

The simple controls and extra features make driving easy and fun. Stay connected with the USB charging port, keep your drinks in the cup holder, and store your things in the special compartment. Plus, the golf ball holder keeps your balls ready.

LED LIGHT

The LED lights are incredibly bright and energy-efficient, improving safety and visibility, particularly in low light. They improve your driving experience while guaranteeing dependability and long-lasting performance thanks to their wider field of vision.

SEAT

Experience the comfort and style with the luxury seat, featuring a two-tone leather design that perfectly blends elegance and relaxation. The striking color contrast adds a touch of sophistication, offering not just exceptional comfort, but a refined, premium look that enhances every drive.

TIRE

Elevate your ride with this 10-inch tire, designed to complement alloy rims and color-matched inserts. Its flat tread design ensures maximum stability and grip on any surface, delivering confident, precise handling and outstanding performance every time you hit the road.

TOGGLE CLAMP

The cargo box features a durable toggle clamp, ensuring secure storage and stability during transport. Quick to lock and unlock, it provides hassle-free access while keeping your tools and equipment safe, making your work more efficient and organized.
