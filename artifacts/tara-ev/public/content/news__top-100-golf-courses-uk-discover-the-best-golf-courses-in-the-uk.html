Top 100 Golf Courses UK: Discover the Best Golf Courses in the UK

The UK has always held a pivotal position in the world of golf. From historic Scottish courses to picturesque English countryside courses, the UK’s golf courses are revered by players worldwide. In recent years, more and more players and travelers have sought comprehensive and authoritative recommendations for the top UK golf courses. For enthusiasts, this list is more than just a venue for a round; it’s an excellent opportunity to deeply experience British culture, nature, and the spirit of sport. This article will reveal the characteristics and highlights of the UK’s top 100 golf courses and answer some frequently asked questions to help you plan your next perfect golf trip.

Why the UK is a mecca for golf courses

The UK is known as the birthplace of golf, and Scotland, in particular, is considered the heart of the sport. From the legendary St. Andrews to Royal Liverpool and Royal Birkdale, nearly every course included in the top 100 golf courses UK is steeped in history and legendary tournaments. Furthermore, the UK boasts a diverse landscape, including links, parkland, and coastal courses, catering to players of all skill levels.

Representatives of the UK’s Top 100 Golf Courses

1. St Andrews Old Course (Scotland)

Known as the “Home of Golf,” this course is a must-try for any golfer.

2. Royal Birkdale (England)

Famous for its demanding fairways and strong winds, it’s a frequent venue for the British Open.

3. Muirfield (Scotland)

One of the world’s oldest golf clubs, it offers a challenging layout.

4. Royal County Down (Northern Ireland)

Rated one of the world’s most scenic golf courses.

5. Royal Dornoch Golf Club (Championship Course) — Scotland

This classic Old Tom Morris layout impresses with its treacherous greens and naturally undulating terrain.

6. Royal Portrush Golf Club (Dunluce Links) — Northern Ireland

A renowned venue for the Open, the renowned Dunluce Links is known for its captivating castle backdrop and challenging design.

7. Carnoustie (Championship Course) — Scotland

Recognized as one of the most challenging championship courses, this course tests both your skills and your mental abilities.

8. Sunningdale Golf Club (Old Course) — England

Designed by Willie Parker, this pine pasture-style course is a prime example of both strategy and playfulness.

9. Sunningdale Golf Club (New Course) — England

Designed by Harry Colt, this course offers five short, unforgettable holes.

10. Royal St George’s — England

A unique blend of dynamic winds and a captivating backdrop.

11. Royal Liverpool (Hoylake) — England

A traditional links course steeped in history, a testament to the depth of a golfer’s skill.

These courses not only represent the quality of top golf courses in the UK, but also showcase the UK’s rich golfing heritage.

Popular Questions

1. Where are the best golf courses in the UK?

The UK’s best courses tend to be concentrated in Scotland and England. Scotland’s St Andrews Old Course and Muirfield consistently rank among the top UK golf courses, while England’s Royal Birkdale and Sunningdale are also highly regarded by players.

2. Can tourists play at top UK courses?

Most courses are open to the public and require advance booking. However, some private clubs may require a member’s invitation. Therefore, when planning a visit to a top UK golf course, it’s recommended to check the relevant booking rules in advance.

3. How much does it cost to play a round at a top UK course?

Prices vary by course. Standard fees at St Andrews range from around £150 to £200, while some high-end private courses can exceed £300. Overall, the fees for the top 100 UK golf courses are in the upper mid-range globally.

4. When is the best season to play golf in the UK?

May to September is the best time of year, with relatively mild weather and long hours of sunshine. Scotland, in particular, offers the unique charm of links golf.
