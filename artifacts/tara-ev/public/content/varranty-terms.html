WARRANTY TERMS

LIMITED VEHICLE WARRANTY

TARA Electric Vehicle proudly offers a comprehensive warranty package to our authorized dealers. When purchasing products from us, they will get a one (1) year warranty on the whole cart from the date of receiving, ensuring quality and peace of mind. Additionally, we provide a generous 8-year warranty specifically for the lithium battery, assuring its long-lasting performance. Please be aware that this warranty does not cover certain special circumstances. For more detailed information, we encourage you to reach out to our dedicated sales or after-sales technicians.
